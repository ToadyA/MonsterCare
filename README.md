Oatkylosaurus is a Tamagotchi-inspired game featuring a dino which is quite similar to an Ankylosaurus, but his tail has a bowl on the end, perfect for holding oats!
There are eight stats to manage:
  Cash is displayed on the right, and can be used to purchase Food (Oatmeal, Bell Pepper, Ice), or Furniture (everything else). See what you can buy by Left-Clicking the "Shopping!" icon at the bottom of the screen.
  Age is also displayed on the right, but it has no urgency to it. By Left-Clicking the "Work..." icon, you may start a shift or end a shift at the Oat Factory. Upon ending a shift, time will pass, causing stats to be affected by the passage of time! Age will increase once every 9 shifts, and will change the Oatkylosaurus's appearance accordingly.
  The Hunger meter on the left can be satisfied by feeding your dino. Left-Click the "Mealtime!" icon to bring up your pantry (and freezer if you have it), then Left-Click what you'd like to feed your boy! He doesn't like peppers as much, but they're more filling.
  Energy can be worked out by playing with your Oatkylosaurus! Left-Click the "Playtime!" icon to chuck a ball at his unsuspecting face. This will also alleviate Boredom, but will have some negative effects if you throw a ball at him while he is asleep.
  Happiness is gained through feeding the Oatkylosaurus yummy oats, and by getting better sleep, which can be achieved by keeping your other stats in good favor.
  Boredom can be alleviated by feeding your boy some Ice, or by throwing the ball at him. Either works, but Ice is only one cent for 10.
While shopping, you can Left-Click to purchase an item, or you can Right-Click an item to hear the Shopkeeper say one thing about that item. Purchasing the Freezer gains you access to Ice, and purchasing the Bookcase gains you access to books.
Once you are done with your Pantry or the Shop or a shift at Work, you can Left-Click that respective icon to close the window, or you can press Esc to close all open windows at once.
Right-Clicking the Oatkylosaurus will prompt him to talk about himself. Right-Clicking any Furniture you own will prompt him to say some five unique thoughts about that thing.
Furniture can be Left-Clicked to interact with it, barring a few exceptions. Books can be read to spend some energy and gain some Sleepiness/Happiness, though reading more than one chapter of a given book on a given day is unbearable.

If your Hunger is full out, or your Happiness is 0 or your Boredom is Full, your HP will fall by one stage, as reflected in the profile picture at the bottom. You can raise this rating by remaining in better standing at the end of a shift.
There is no consequence for existing or remaining at 0 HP, beyond feeling bad for neglecting your boy.
